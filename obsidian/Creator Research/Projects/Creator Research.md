# Creator Research

## Purpose
This project researches publicly available information about content creators, particularly creators involved in AI, software, technology, and AI-generated content.

The goal is to discover relevant creators, collect publicly available information, analyze their content, and maintain structured research records.

## Stack
- AI agent: [[Hermes]]
- Reasoning/coding model: DeepSeek
- Scraping & data processing: Python
- Knowledge base: [[Obsidian]] (this vault)
- Crawling: Playwright (existing), Crawl4AI 0.9.3 (tested, available)
