# Gianluca Mauro

## Profile

Gianluca Mauro is an AI entrepreneur, educator, and speaker. He is the founder of AI Academy, a leading AI education and transformation company that has taught AI to thousands of individuals and teams at companies like Scania, P&G, Daikin, Merck, and Turner Construction.

He also founded Epiphany, an AI tool used by Fortune 500 companies for internal training. He teaches at Harvard's Executive Education school and is a TEDx speaker ("Why AI is more human than you think").

His TikTok content focuses on AI news, business applications of AI, and AI education. He has 171.4K TikTok followers.
